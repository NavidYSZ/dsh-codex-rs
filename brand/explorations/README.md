# Mark explorations

Three directions were drawn as SVG and judged on the same page at 256, 96, 64,
32, 29 and 16 px, on light and dark, in a browser-tab mock, in the sidebar next
to the name and inside a 58 px / 29 px app-icon body (the scratch pages are not
kept; the three SVGs here are the directions as submitted).

| File | Direction | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `01-ae-monogram.svg` | Æ monogram: the A's right leg is the E's stem, the crossbar is the middle bar | Strongest link to the name, but it is text: at 16 px the bars merge into a smudge, and the brief excludes letters inside the mark. Rejected. |
| `02-horizon-arc.svg` | The upper air: a horizon arc, a point above it, a glow between | The story the name asks for, but a point above an arc reads as a *person* (head over shoulders) when centred and as a *frown with one eye* when offset; a shallow horizon still read as a person at 64 px, and moving the point under the arc turned it into Wi‑Fi or an eye. Rejected after some twenty variants (centred, offset, shallow, steep, point under the arc, framed). |
| `03-orbit-ring.svg` | An orbit: a ring with a satellite point | Chosen, with the point moved **onto** the ring. A ring with the point outside (the Oxygen mark) reads as ♂ at 16 px; an open ring with the point in the gap reads as a loading spinner; a point at the top reads as a jewellery ring. A closed ring with the point sitting on it at 45° reads as a body on its orbit at every size and is not a face, a spinner or a symbol. |

## Rationale for the chosen direction

Aether is, in the classical picture, the substance of the heavens: the element
the celestial spheres are made of and in which everything above the moon moves.
The mark draws exactly that: a ring, the sphere of the upper air, and a point on
it, the thing that moves in it (the agent reaching the phone, the phone reaching
the Mac, over the aether). It is one shape in one colour (ring and point share a
fill; the glow is optional and lives only on the app icon), it keeps the
continuity the user already liked in the Oxygen mark (a geometric stroke plus a
point, like Flai's curve and Aimmo's lambda), it reads at 16 px as a ring with a
body on it and at 29 px with room to spare, and it does not collapse into a
face, a person, a spinner or a gender symbol, which is where every horizon and
open-ring variant ended. Construction: `../BRAND.md` § The mark.
